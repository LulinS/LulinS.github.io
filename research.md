<h1 class="page">Research</h1>

My research examines how regulation and disclosure shape social outcomes. Across three settings — carbon disclosure, mortgage lending, and healthcare pricing — I ask when transparency rules deliver on their promise and when they shift harm onto the people they were meant to protect.
